🐔 SPICY CHICKEN

Description:

   Spicy Chicken is a flavorful dish made with tender chicken cooked in chili, garlic, and soy sauce.

It’s hot, tasty, and best served with rice!

🍴 Ingredients:

  1. 500g chicken (cut into small pieces)
  2. 3 cloves garlic (chopped)
  3. 1 onion (sliced)
  4. 2 tbsp soy sauce
  5. 1 tbsp vinegar
  6. 2 red chilies (chopped)
  7. 1 tbsp cooking oil
  8. Salt and pepper to taste
  9. (Optional) Chopped spring onions for garnish

    10. 🔥 Instructions:

  10. Heat oil in a pan.
  11. Sauté garlic and onion until fragrant.
  12. Add chicken and cook until light brown.
  13. Pour soy sauce and vinegar, then mix well.
  14. Add chilies, salt, and pepper.
  15. Cook for 10–15 minutes or until chicken is tender.
  16. Garnish with spring onions.
  17. Serve hot and enjoy your spicy chicken!
